Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information TaxWizards collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies only to this website and the enquiry form on it.

Information we collect

When you submit the enquiry form, we collect the details you enter:

  • your name;
  • your phone number;
  • your email address;
  • the service you are interested in;
  • any message or requirement you choose to share.

We do not ask for financial account numbers, government identifiers or other sensitive information through this form. Please do not include them in your message.

This site does not use advertising cookies or analytics tracking. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and request time, for security and reliability.

To keep the enquiry form free of automated abuse, we use Cloudflare Turnstile. It may process your IP address, browser information and limited device data, and may store essential cookies or similar identifiers, solely to distinguish people from bots. It is not used for advertising or analytics.

How we use your information

  • to respond to your enquiry and contact you about it;
  • to understand which service you need and provide the information you request;
  • to keep records of enquiries and our responses;
  • to meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations that apply to our work.

By submitting the form, you consent to being contacted about your enquiry. You can withdraw that consent at any time by telling us when we contact you.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate this website and respond to enquiries, such as our hosting provider, our form-delivery provider, Formspree, and our bot-protection provider, Cloudflare Turnstile. They receive only what is needed to perform that service. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

When you submit the form, your details are processed by our form-delivery provider and sent to our business inbox.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond, maintain our records and meet legal, tax or regulatory requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have shared, or ask us to stop contacting you. Use the enquiry form and mention “Privacy request” in your message. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

For any question about this policy or the way your information is handled, email us at info@taxwizards.in or call +91 84606 22620, and mention “Privacy request”. You can also use the enquiry form.
